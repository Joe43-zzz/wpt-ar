What you see

"Modify" is a thought experiment: add a resonant capacitor or relay coil to the real device on screen and see how the lighting range, energy path and efficiency change. Drag sideways on the scope to freeze time and step through one cycle.

"3D" turns the tracked setup into a model you rotate with a finger; it follows the coils when they move. "Range" draws where on the table the receiver lights the LED.

The scope is in slow motion: induced voltage peaks when the current changes fastest, a quarter cycle behind the flux; red marks when the LED conducts. The real current oscillates many thousands of times a second. Field mode shows one slice through the coil axis facing the camera: lines grow out of the coil as the current rises and shrink as it falls; lines through the receiver glow brighter, with a dot where they pierce it. Energy mode shows the path energy takes through the air; it flows only while the LED conducts.

Model limits: field lines show the transmitter field only; with a relay the energy path is drawn in two stages; efficiency covers the coils only, not the oscillator; wire and LED resistance are estimates; V₂ is an open-circuit estimate scaled by a calibration factor fitted to the real LED; powers are relative.
